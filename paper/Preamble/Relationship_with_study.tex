\section*{\Huge\centering{Relationship Between Prior Publications and This Thesis}}

\vspace{0.5cm}
\large

This thesis integrates and extends five peer-reviewed works produced during the doctoral programme, on each of which the author is first author. Table~\ref{tab:publication-thesis-mapping} maps each work to the chapters that draw on it and identifies the material introduced in the thesis beyond the published version. The trust-partitioning definition and the verifiability theorem (Section~\ref{subsec:strata-theorem}), the anchoring-rate and revocation-delay propositions, the decay-based trust law with its streak trigger (Section~\ref{sec:plane-a}), the role-based definition of a faithful instantiation (Section~\ref{sec:strata-substrates}), and the consolidated answers to the research questions (Table~\ref{tab:rq-answers}) are original to this thesis and have not previously been published.
